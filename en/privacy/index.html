Privacy Policy

Funtopia stores your phone number or previous username, name, instrument, rehearsal attendance and readiness, uploaded photos, videos and audio, messages, reports and blocks. Ensemble members can access this data within their ensemble. Passwords are stored as hashes and your session is stored on your device in Keychain.

Your location is used on your device to find a route to rehearsals and is not saved to your profile. Camera, photo library, microphone and local network access are requested when you use the corresponding feature. Other features remain available if you decline.

Your response to the ATT system prompt is saved on your device. The app shows no ads, does not share IDFA and does not track activity across apps. The APNs device token is not sent to the backend or third parties.

You can delete your account and its associated data in Settings.
