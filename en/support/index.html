Open the next rehearsal, select your part, listen to a recording and mark yourself as ready in the lineup. For shared playback, open the player on two devices on the same local network and tap Find devices.

You can change microphone, camera, location or local network permissions in iOS Settings. Alternative actions are described on each screen.

Use the three-dot menu to report material or block its author. Account deletion is available in Profile and Settings.
