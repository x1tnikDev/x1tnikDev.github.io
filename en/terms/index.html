Terms of Service

Funtopia helps ensemble members prepare for rehearsals and share part materials. New accounts use a phone number and password. Existing accounts can also use their previous username. You are responsible for the materials you upload and must have permission to share them.

Do not publish recordings or sheet music without permission, abusive material, spam or illegal content. You can report recordings, comments, messages or profiles and block their authors. Content that violates the rules may be hidden.

You can delete your account and related data in Settings. Local network features require devices to be connected to the same network. Notifications depend on iOS permission. Server push delivery is unavailable in this version.
